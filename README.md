# PRODIGY_DS_05
## Task-05: Traffic Accident Analysis

### Objective
Analyze traffic accident data to identify patterns related to road conditions, weather, and time of day. Visualize accident hotspots and contributing factors.

### Dataset
- Source: US Accidents dataset (4.2M records, 49 states).
- Key columns: `Start_Time`, `Weather_Condition`, `Amenity`, `Crossing`, `Side`, `Severity`.

### Visualizations
- Accidents by weather condition.
- Accidents by road features.
- Accidents by time of day.

### Insights
- Certain weather conditions (rain, fog, snow) correlate with higher accident counts.
- Road features like crossings and intersections show elevated risk.
- Peak accident times occur during morning and evening rush hours.

### Conclusion
Traffic accident analysis highlights how **environmental factors** and **time of day** contribute to risk.  
These insights can guide **road safety policies**, **driver awareness campaigns**, and **infrastructure improvements**.
