## Task-05: Traffic Accident Analysis

### Objective
Analyze traffic accident data to identify patterns related to road conditions, weather, and time of day. Visualize accident hotspots and contributing factors.

### Dataset
- Source: US Accidents dataset (March 2023 version).
- Sample size: 100,000 rows (to keep analysis efficient).
- Key columns: `Start_Time`, `Weather_Condition`, `Crossing`, `Start_Lat`, `Start_Lng`.

### Insights
- **Weather:** Most accidents occur in clear weather, suggesting traffic density and driver behavior are stronger factors than adverse weather.  
- **Road Features:** Crossings and intersections show elevated accident counts, highlighting risk at traffic conflict points.  
- **Time of Day:** Accidents peak during morning (around 11 AM) and evening (around 8 PM) rush hours, with lows between 3–6 AM.

### Conclusion
Traffic accident analysis reveals how **environmental factors** and **time of day** contribute to risk.  
These insights can guide **road safety policies**, **driver awareness campaigns**, and **infrastructure improvements**.
